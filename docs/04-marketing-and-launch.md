# Marketing & Launch

Write `docs/marketing-plan.md` (from `templates/marketing-plan.md`) as
soon as the build is functional and passing CI — before launch, not after.

## Channel priority on a near-zero budget

Ranked by realistic ROI, cheapest and most durable first:

| Channel | Cost | Time to impact | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| ASO foundation (title, subtitle, keywords) | $0 (your time) | 4–8 weeks | ~60% of App Store downloads come from search; compounds, do this first |
| Store screenshots (benefit-led) | $0 DIY | Immediate on listing | First screenshot decides whether someone explores or bounces |
| Short-form organic video (TikTok/Reels) | $0 (your time) | Days–weeks | High but volatile |
| Communities / build-in-public | $0 (your time) | Ongoing | Compounds slowly, good fit for a portfolio approach |
| Launch window (Product Hunt, HN, deal-trackers) | $0 + optional price drop | One spike | Fades fast, useful for a download-velocity signal at launch |
| Reviews & retention flywheel | $0 (your time) | Ongoing | Ratings/reviews and engagement both feed ranking |
| Paid (Apple Search Ads, Meta) | $$ | Weeks to stabilize | Last, once the funnel already converts |

## Launch timeline (4–5 weeks, standard shape)

- **T-4 weeks**: App Store Connect metadata, privacy nutrition labels,
  screenshots, App Store Optimization keyword research (aim for 5–10
  realistic keywords, not just high-volume ones).
- **T-3 weeks**: TestFlight beta to a small pool; fix crashes and
  onboarding friction before it's public.
- **T-2 weeks**: Submit to App Store. Budget 24–48h for review, but leave
  a buffer for rejection/resubmission (metadata and privacy-disclosure
  issues are the most common rejection reasons).
- **T-1 week**: Prepare launch-day comms — waitlist email, social posts,
  Product Hunt assets — pre-approved and scheduled, not written on the
  day.
- **Launch day**: submit to Product Hunt (if relevant to the category),
  send the waitlist email with store links, post to any communities
  you're already part of.

## ASO checklist (the free, compounding channel)

- App name: primary keyword alongside the brand name (30-char limit on
  iOS) — e.g. "Verso: Photo Culling" beats "Verso" alone.
- Subtitle: 1–2 supporting keywords, not a repeat of the title.
- 5 screenshots, portrait, captioned: first screenshot carries the
  single biggest benefit in large text.
- Optional ~30s preview video, silent-by-default, showing the core flow.
- Description written for humans (Apple doesn't index it for search, but
  people read it before installing).

## What "maximise uptake" actually means at this budget

Given the channel table above, "maximise uptake" for a solo-dev budget
means: get the ASO foundation right before launch (it's the compounding
one), spend real design effort on the first screenshot, and treat the
launch-day spike (Product Hunt etc.) as a download-velocity signal to the
App Store algorithm rather than the main acquisition channel — paid
acquisition doesn't carry an indie app in 2026 the way it might have
years ago.
